We thank the referee for the valuable comments. \textcolor{black}{We have addressed all of them.} We list the corrections/modifications that we implemented following the referee's comments.

\smallskip

\begin{enumerate}

\item We have addressed this point in the proof of Theorem $3.1$. The bottom row of the commutative diagram of exact sequences do indeed reduce to what we had claimed before, but we found it more convenient to directly argue using the commutative diagram 

\[
\begin{tikzcd}
  0 \arrow[r] & \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^1} \arrow[r, "\sigma_{\mathbb{P}^1}"] \arrow[d, "\Bar{w}", "\cong"'] & \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^1}(1)^{\oplus 2} \arrow[r, "\rho_{\mathbb{P}^1}"] \arrow[d, "\widetilde{\alpha}"] & T_{\mathbb{P}^1} \arrow[r] \arrow[d, "\beta = di"] & 0 \\
   0 \arrow[r] & \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^1} \arrow[r, "i^*\sigma_{\mathbb{P}^M}"] & \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^1}(2)^{M+1} \arrow[r, "i^*\rho_{\mathbb{P}^M}"] & T_{\mathbb{P}^2}|_{\mathbb{P}^1} \oplus \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}^1}(2)^{M-2} = T_{\mathbb{P}^M|_{\mathbb{P}^1}} \arrow[r] & 0
\end{tikzcd}
\]

All the local computations are shown in Lemma $3.4$

\item Theorem $4.4$ of \cite{BMR21} has been cited in the proof of Theorem $2.4$.

\item \cite[Lemma $2.6$, (iii)]{BMR21} has been changed to \cite[Lemma $2.5$, $(4)$]{BMR21} and \cite[Lemma $2.5$ (iii)] {BMR21} has been changed to \cite[Lemma $2.5$, $(3)$]{BMR21} at the end of the proof of Theorem $3.1$.

\item $a = 1$ has been changed to $a = 2$ in item $(5)$ of the proof of Theorem $3.11$.

\item A period is added after $8k+16$ after item $(5)$ of the proof of Theorem $3.11$.

\item A period is added after $1$ in item $(7)$ of Theorem $3.15$.

\item We have included the correct bounds as pointed out by the referee in items $(3)$ and $(4)$ of Proposition $4.1$. We thank the referee for giving us the correct bounds (indeed the line bundle $H = \mathcal{O}_Y(aC_0+bf)$ needs to be very ample first !).

\item All occurences of $N_{\widetilde{Y}/\mathbb{P}^N}$ in Proposition $4.1$ have been replaced by $N_{\widetilde{Y}/\mathbb{P}^M}$.

\item A period has been added after $1209$ in the proof of Theorem $4.6$.


\end{enumerate}
